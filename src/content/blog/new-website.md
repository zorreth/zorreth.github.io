**Hi there everybody!**

The zorreth.com website just got a huge update. I added a bit more information about me, the blog section, and the overall user experience got better.

Just like my previous website, this one was built using Astro. No libraries, everything is being pre-generated at the build time. The design was greatly inspired by the [WhiteHoodHacker.net](https://whitehoodhacker.net) website. I didn't use any of his code, just really liked the design :)

I heard Astro is amazing for this kind of websites, that's why I decided to try it. The blog posts are just markdown files, Astro translates them into pages automatically, which works great for me. I am finally happy with the result, and I plan on keeping this website and blog alive.
